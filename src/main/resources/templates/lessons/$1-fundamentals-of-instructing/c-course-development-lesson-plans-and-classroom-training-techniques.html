Foundations of Teaching

CFI's must be able to organize information in a way that learners will comprehend.

Process

  • Preparation
  • Presentation
  • Application
  • Review / Assessment

Essential Instructor Skills

  • Management Skills - plan, organize, lead, supervise
  • Assessment Skills - verify learner progress
  • People Skills - relate to learners, communicate effectively
  • Subject Matter Expertise - comprehensive, deep understanding of required material

Course Design and Planning

Course

  • The overall goal.
  • Syllabus and Lessons serve to reach this goal.
  • Example: PPL 141 Course

Syllabus

Structured sequence of objectives that add up to the course goal.

  • road map
  • arranges building blocks of learning in logical order
  • well defined objectives and standards of completion
  • flexible

Lesson Plans

Organized structure for a single instruction period.

  • objectives
  • content
  • completion standards

Building Blocks

  • discrete skill or knowledge item that can be practiced individually
  • can be practiced individually
  • combined with other building blocks to achieve larger objectives

Lesson Objectives and Preparation

Begin with Performance Based Objectives, then gradually change to Decision Based Objectives.

Performance Based Objectives

The learner is expected to perform an exact procedure to a specific standard.

  • description - measurable desired outcome
  • conditions - rules, parameters, tools
  • criteria - standards measuring objective completion

Decision Based Objectives

  • higher level of learning
  • builds on performance based objectives (learn, then apply)

Training Delivery Methods

Lecture
  • present new material to a large audience
Discussion
  • short lecture followed by discussion between learners and instructors or other learners
Guided Discussion
  • goal is to encourage participation and application of skills
  • more engaging
  • instructor is a facilitator
Cooperative of Group Learning
  • organize learners into small groups
  • clear specific objectives
Demonstration-Performance Method
  • useful for specific skills that require practice
  • explanation, demonstration, learner performance, instructor supervision, evaluation
Drill and Practice Method
  • strengthen skill with practice

Technology and Instructional Aids

Electronic Learning

  • time flexible
  • cost effective
  • lack of personal feedback and interaction from instructor

Instructional Aids

  • simple, factual, straightforward
  • clarify relationships
  • hold learner's attention
