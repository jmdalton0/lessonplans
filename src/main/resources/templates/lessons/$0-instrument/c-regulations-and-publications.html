Instrument Procedures - Departures

IFR procedures to guide aircraft from takeoff to en route

Standard Instrument Departure (SID)

  • can be radar vectors, RNAV, or a combination
  • can be graphical or textual

ODPs

  • similar to SIDs, but require non-standard climb gradients
  • indicated by "Obstacle" in the title

Diverse Vector Area (DVA)

  • radar vectors can be assigned below the MVA

Visual Climb Over Airport

  • used when unable to make climb gradient
  • ATC clearance required (request on initial contact)
  • minimums listed on procedure
  • remain within visibility minimums distance of airport

Instrument Procedures - Approaches

Precision Approaches

  • lateral and vertical guidance
  • DA or DH - decide to continue or go missed
Instrument Landing System (ILS)
  • localizer (LOC)
  • glideslope (GS)
  • approach lighting system (ALS)
  • marker beacons
Ground Based Augmentation System (GBAS) Landing System (GLS)
  • ground based system that receives GPS signals
  • sends corrections to aircraft
Precision Approach Radar
  • ATC uses radar to provide verbal guidance

Instrument Procedures - Approaches

Non-Precision Approaches

  • lateral guidance, NO vertical guidance
  • uses minimum altitudes or "stepdowns"
  • MDA - continue to missed approach point before deciding to go missed
NDB Approach
VOR Approach
Localizer (LOC)
  • Localizer-Type Directional Aid (LDA)
Localizer Performance (LP)
  • PBN version of a LOC
LNAV
  • LNAV+V

Instrument Procedures - Approaches

Approach with Vertical Guidance (APV)

  • PBN approaches with lateral and vertical guidance
  • DA or DH - decide to continue or go missed
  • Legally NOT considered Precision Approaches
Localizer Performance with Vertical Guidance (LPV)
  • PBN version of an ILS
LNAV/VNAV
  • can be flown with WAAS or Baro-aided
Required Navigation Performance (RNP)
  • RNP-AR (Authorization Required)
LDA/GS
  • final approach course is not aligned with the runway
  • vertical guidance provided, but does NOT meet Precision Approach criteria
