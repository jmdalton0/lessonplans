Area of Operation II.A

Objective

  • The learner will understand function and use of aircraft instruments and navigation equipment.
  • The learner will develop skills to calibrate, operate, and interpret flight deck instruments and apply these skills to instrument flying.
  • The learner will mitigate risk by understanding common errors and failures associated with aircraft and ground based equipment and learning to respond to accordingly.
