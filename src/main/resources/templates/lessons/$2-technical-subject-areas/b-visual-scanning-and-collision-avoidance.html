Area of Operation II.B

Objective

  • The learner will understand the importance and responsibility of maintaining situational awareness and visually scanning for collision hazards during VFR flight.
  • The learner will develop skills to maintain visual separation with hazards.
  • The learner will mitigate risk by developing habits and skills to maintain visual separation with hazards.

Scanning for Other Aircraft

  • day vs night (fovea vs peripherals)
  • short, regularly spaced eye movements
  • aircraft that appear to have no relative motion are likely on a collision course

Right-of-way Rules

Anytime
  • aircraft in distress
When Converging
  • balloons
  • gliders
  • airship
  • towing or refueling
Head On
  • both yield to the right
Overtaking
  • slower aircraft have right-of-way
  • but expect to be passed on the right
Landing
  • landing aircraft have right-of-way
  • can't force people off the runway
  • if two are landing, lower altitude has right-of-way
