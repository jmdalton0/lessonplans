Area of Operation II.M

Objective

  • The learner will understand human factors, lighting, and operations that apply to night flight.
  • The learner will develop skills to legally and safely prepare for and fly at night.
  • The learner will mitigate risk by recognizing and responding to adverse situations possible during night flight.
