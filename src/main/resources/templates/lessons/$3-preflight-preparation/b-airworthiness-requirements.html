Area of Operation III.B

Objective

  • The learner will understand airworthiness as it applies to aircraft and how to verify and aircraft is worthy of flight.
  • The learner will develop skills to verify that an aircraft is safe and legal to fly, as well as steps to take if it is not.
  • The learner will mitigate risk by understanding the importance of ensuring an aircraft is airworthy.

Required Inspections

General

Annual Inspection
  • every 12 calendar months
  • A&P with Inspection Authorization (IA)
100 Hour Inspection
  • every 100 engine hours
  • an annual can be used as a 100 hour
  • for hire only
  • can exceed 100 hours by 10 hours if en route to inspection location
Transponder
  • every 24 calendar months
ELT (Emergency Locator Transmitter)
  • 121.5
  • every 12 calendar months
  • 1 hour of use
  • half of the useful battery life

IFR Only

VOR
  • every 30 days
  • can be performed by pilot
Static System
  • every 24 calendar months
