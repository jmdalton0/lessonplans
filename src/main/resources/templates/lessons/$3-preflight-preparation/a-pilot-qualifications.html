Area of Operation III.A

Pilot Qualifications

  • The learner will understand the applicable certification and experience requirements for each of their flights.
  • The learner will develop skills to determine if their privileges, limitations, experience, documentation, etc. is valid for the kind of flying they will be doing.
  • The learner will mitigate risk by recognizing whether they can legally and safely complete a flight.
