Atmospheric Composition and Stability

Atmospheric Composition

  • Nitrogen - 78%
  • Oxygen - 21%
  • Other - 1%

Atmospheric Layers

  • Troposphere
    • surface to about FL360 on average
    • temperature decreases with height
    • most weather happens below the tropopause
  • Stratosphere
    • temperature increases with height
    • commercial aircraft cruise here to avoid turbulence
  • Mesosphere
    • decrease in temperature with height
  • Thermosphere
    • temperature increases with height
  • Exosphere

Standard Atmosphere

  • 15°C / 59°F
  • 29.92" Hg / 1013.25 mb

Lapse Rate

  • standard environmental lapse rate - 2°C per 1000 ft
  • dry adiabatic lapse rate - 3°C per 1000 ft
  • saturate adiabatic lapse rate - 1.5°C per 1000 ft

Temperature Inversion

  • increase in temperature with and increase of altitude
  • radiation inversion or frontal inversion
  • poor visibility
  • smooth air
  • wind shear across the boundary

Air Masses and Fronts

Air Mass

large body of air with generally uniform temperature and humidity

Fronts

Warm Front
  • 10 - 25 mph
  • various cloud types, cumulonimbus in the summer
  • light precip
  • low then improving visibility
  • temperature rises
  • pressure rises then falls
Cold Front
  • 25 - 60 mph
  • cumulonimbus clouds are common if air is unstable
  • showery precip
  • low visibility
  • temperature falls
  • pressure falls then rises
Stationary Front
  • mix of weather associated with warm and cold fronts
Occluded Front
  • Cold Front Occlusion
    • overtaking air mass is colder
    • mix of weather association with warm and cold fronts
  • Warm Front Occlusion
    • overtaken air mass is colder
    • weather more sever than a cold front if unstable

AIRMETs, SIGMETs, and Convective SIGMETs

AIRMET

  • 6 hr
    • SIERRA - mountain obscuration
    • TANGO - turbulence
    • ZULU - icing

SIGMET

  • 4 hr
  • severe icing, extreme turbulence, sandstorms, volcanic ash

Conv SIGMET

  • 2 hr
  • severe, embedded, or squall line thunderstorms
  • surface winds greater than 50 knots
  • hail greater than 3/4 in
  • tornadoes
